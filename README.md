# Thauma-Geared

Overgeared-style smithing for Thaumaturge's thaumium and void metal (NeoForge 1.21.1). Requires Overgeared and Thaumaturge.

- **Arcane Anvil** (Магическая наковальня): Overgeared's Tier A anvil in dark purple with a gold rim. Sprinkle Salis Mundus on an Overgeared iron smithing anvil to make one (needs the *Metallurgy* research).
- Thaumium and void metal ingots are heated (glowing blue) only in Thaumaturge's Infernal Furnace and forged on the Arcane Anvil into Overgeared-style tool heads (sword blade, pickaxe head, axe head, shovel head, hoe head), which are then joined to a stick.
- Plates are hammered on the Arcane Anvil, and armour is forged from those plates.
- Thaumaturge's crafting-table recipes for these tools, armour pieces and plates are replaced.

`tools/gen.py` generates the textures, models, names and recipes.

**Hands-On Overgeared:** with it installed, the Arcane Anvil works like every other Overgeared anvil: you lay the pieces on the anvil and strike in rhythm with the hammer in your hand. No extra setup is needed.

**Smithing Guide (Patchouli, optional):** a book with an iron ingot makes a guide to Overgeared's mechanics (hammers, anvils, heat and tongs, forging, quality, blueprints, polishing, quenching, assembly, steel, alloys, casting) and the Arcane Anvil, in English and Russian. `tools/gen_book.py` writes it.
